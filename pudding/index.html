Mallang Pudding

This needs WebGPU

The puddings are simulated on the CPU and rendered with raw WebGPU shaders (soft shadows, contact shading, skinned soft bodies). There is no WebGL fallback, so the scene can't start here.

Checking…

Try a current Chrome, Edge or Arc on desktop or Android, or Safari 26+. On Linux Chrome you may need chrome://flags/#enable-unsafe-webgpu. The page must be served over https or from localhost.
